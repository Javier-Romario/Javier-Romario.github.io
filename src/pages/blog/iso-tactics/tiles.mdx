## The cube recipe

The top face is the same diamond, but shifted <b>up</b> by the height. Then draw three visible
faces: left (dark), right (mid), top (bright). Light comes from the top — that shading is what
tricks your brain into seeing 3D.

<Formula math="topY = (x + y) × H/2 − height" note="Same diamond, lifted up by the block height." />

```js
// three faces, in order:
// 1. left face  (darkest)  — connects left edge of top + bottom diamonds
// 2. right face (middle)   — connects right edge
// 3. top face   (bright)   — the lifted diamond
```

## Why this matters

Units, walls, buildings — in a 2D iso game they're all just cubes (or boxes) with a height.
Draw them with `x + y` depth sort (step 05) and you have a scene.
