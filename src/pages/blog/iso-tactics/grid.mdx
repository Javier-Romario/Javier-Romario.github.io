## Manhattan distance

Movement on a grid is not a straight line — you walk tile to tile. The distance between two
tiles is how many steps you take, moving up/down/left/right only. That's **Manhattan distance**
(named after city blocks — no diagonals through buildings).

<Formula math="d = |x₁−x₂| + |y₁−y₂|" note="Add the x-difference and y-difference. That's the step count." />

## Why this matters

Later you'll use this same number for:
- **Move range** — "which tiles are within 3 steps?"
- **Attack range** — "is the enemy 1 step away?"
- **A\*** — the pathfinding heuristic (step 07).
